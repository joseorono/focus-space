# Screenshot bullets — short copy beside each screenshot

The popup is ~500px wide on a 1280×800 canvas. That leaves about 700px of side space — enough for **one short headline + 2-3 micro-bullets**. Anything more and the screenshot becomes unreadable at thumbnail size.

Rules of thumb:
- **Headline:** 3-6 words.
- **Bullets:** 4-7 words each. 3 max. Lead with an icon.
- **No paragraphs.** No sub-bullets. No exclamation marks unless earned.
- Same icon style across all 5 screenshots so the listing feels like one set.

## Brand colors (for the panel background, if you bake bullets into the screenshot)

- Background: `#030e18` (matches the landing page)
- Icon accent: `#60a5fa` (primary-light) or `#8b5cf6` (secondary)
- Headline text: white
- Bullet text: `#94a3b8` or similar muted gray

## Don't

- Use red/error icons (❌ ⛔). They scan as warnings.
- Mix emoji and SVG in the same screenshot.
- Push more than 3 bullets in — at thumbnail size it becomes noise.
- Use "AI-powered." It's keyword-based and that's the strength.
