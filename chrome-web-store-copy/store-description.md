Your browser remembers every distraction. FocusSpace forgets them for you.

One click closes the distracting tabs you didn't mean to leave open. Another click wipes those sites from your history — so your autocomplete stops suggesting them the next time you open a new tab. Then a built-in Pomodoro timer keeps you in the zone, with gentle alerts if a flagged tab sneaks open mid-session.

No account. No cloud. No tracking. Everything runs locally in your browser.

— WHAT IT DOES —

• Session Cleaner — One button closes every tab matching a distraction category (social, gaming, NSFW, shopping, entertainment, dating, health, travel) or your custom keywords. Whitelisted domains stay open.

• Browser Cleaner — Surgically removes history entries by category. Your research, docs, and work links survive. The temptations don't. Your URL autocomplete stops betraying you.

• Focus Mode — A 25/5 Pomodoro timer wired to a task list and session tracking. Real-time alerts if a distracting tab opens during a session. Configurable work, short-break, and long-break durations.

• Smart Settings — Toggle the 8 built-in categories, add custom keywords for personal triggers, and whitelist any domain you can't afford to lose. The whitelist always takes priority.

— WHY IT'S DIFFERENT —

Most "focus" extensions are blockers — they fight your willpower in real time, and you eventually disable them. FocusSpace works before the impulse: it removes the triggers (open tabs, history hints, autocomplete suggestions) that cause the distraction in the first place. Pair it with a blocker if you want — they solve different problems.

— PRIVACY BY ARCHITECTURE —

Every byte of processing happens locally in your browser. No servers. No analytics. No telemetry. No external API calls. The full source is on GitHub under GPLv3 — read it, fork it, contribute. We couldn't see your data even if we wanted to.

— FREE FOREVER —

No paywall. No "pro" tier. No upsell. No email required. Install, configure once, use forever.

GitHub: https://github.com/JoseOroOro/clean-my-history
Made with care by The Exologic Team.
